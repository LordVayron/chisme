# Sponsor Chisme

Chisme is free, with no ads, no account and no telemetry. Sponsors keep it that way.

Sponsoring is a thank-you, not a purchase: every feature stays free for everyone. In return, your
name or logo is shown in the app (the **Support Chisme** page), on the website and in this list.

## Tiers (monthly)

| Tier | Price | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Supporter | $5 | Your name in the app and in this list |
| Backer | $25 | Your name with a link in the app, on the website and here |
| Partner | $100 | Your logo with a link in the app, on the website and here |
| Main | $500 | A large logo at the top of the Support page and the website |

## How to become a sponsor

1. Sponsor through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/LordVayron).
2. Open a [sponsor listing](https://github.com/LordVayron/chisme/issues/new?template=sponsor.yml)
   issue with the name, link and logo you want shown.
3. The list is updated within a few days. The app and website read it from
   [`sponsors.json`](sponsors.json), so no update is needed to see it.
